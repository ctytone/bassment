Loops: the 8 round buttons on the top row are loop tracks. Tap an empty one to record (one bar of count-in when nothing is playing, otherwise it starts at the next bar), and it loops when the bar count you picked is up. Tap a playing loop to mute or unmute it; hold it for a second to delete it. Loops can mix drums, 808s and melody, and keep playing while you change page. Pages: the bottom two round buttons on the right step through the pages. Drums: each row is one kind of sound; the right-hand buttons pick a hi-hat roll speed, then hold any drum pad to roll it in time with the tempo. 808s and Melody: a note plays for as long as you hold its pad; hold several pads to play them together. Melody: real recorded instruments. On the 808 and Melody pages every pad is in the chosen minor key; the brighter pads are the root note. Key, tempo, Stop everything and the light show are in the panel. All sounds are CC0 (free to use in your own music): sound credits.
