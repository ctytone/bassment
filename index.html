Pages: the bottom two round buttons on the right step through Drums, 808s and Melody (Session, User 1 and User 2 on the top row jump straight to them). Drums: each row is one kind of sound; the right-hand buttons pick a hi-hat roll speed, then hold any drum pad to roll it in time with the tempo. 808s and Melody: a note plays for as long as you hold its pad; hold several pads to play them together. Melody: real recorded instruments. On the 808 and Melody pages every pad is in the chosen minor key; the brighter pads are the root note. ◀ ▶ on the top row change the key, ▼ stops every sound.
